Frequently asked questions

What counts as an interview?

A confirmed interview invitation (call, video or in person) from a company for a role matching your agreed target post and city.

When does my deadline start?

On the day we verify your payment. Your exact date appears on your Guarantee Certificate.

How do I pay?

JazzCash or Meezan Bank transfer. Upload your receipt and transaction ID when you apply.

How do I get a refund?

If the window closes without an interview and you met the fair-play conditions, contact support with your Reference ID. You receive your fee minus taxes and surcharges within 5–7 business days.

Can I track progress?

Yes. Enter your Reference ID on the Track Status page.

Is my CV kept private?

Your CV is shared only with vetted hiring managers and deleted on request. See our Privacy Policy.
