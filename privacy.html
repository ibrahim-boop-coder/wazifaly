Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Data we collect

Your name, WhatsApp number, email, career details, CV and payment proof (transaction ID and receipt).

How we use it

To verify payment, prepare your profile, contact you and present you to employers.

Storage and security

Data is stored on access-controlled systems and transferred over HTTPS. Only authorized Wazifly staff can access it.

Sharing

Your CV is shared only with vetted hiring managers and partner companies relevant to your target role. We never sell your data.

Retention and deletion

You may ask us to delete your CV, contact records and payment proofs at any time from the contact page. We delete within 7 business days, except records we must keep for accounting.

Browser storage

The tracking feature saves your Reference ID details in your own browser (localStorage).
