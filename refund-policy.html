The window starts on the day your payment is verified. If Wazifly does not secure you an interview (a confirmed interview invitation for a role matching your agreed target post and city) by the end of the window, you are eligible for a refund.

Fair-play conditions

  • You attend all scheduled interviews.
  • Your role and salary expectations are realistic for the market.
  • Your CV and details are accurate.
  • You respond to Wazifly within 24 hours.
  • You have not declined a matching interview invitation.

How to request a refund

Within 7 days after your window closes, send your Reference ID to support from the contact page.

Refund amount

You receive the fee you paid minus applicable taxes and payment surcharges (for example, JazzCash or bank transfer charges). These deductions are disclosed before you pay.

Payout process

Approved refunds are paid within 5–7 business days to the original payment method or the account you nominate.
